  • Unjumble words & write Caption for the Cartoon.
  • Cartoon Area – Tap to zoom, tap again to close.
  • Jumble Area – Tap a word to move to Work Area.
  • Work Area – Tap syllables in order to form the word.
  • HL Pool – Collects important syllables for the caption.
  • Caption Area – Arrange HL syllables to form the final caption.
